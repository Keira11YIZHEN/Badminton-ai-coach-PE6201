# External context checked for the problem framing

Checked **2 October 2026**. These sources are used only to avoid claiming the problem category is unserved and to provide a concrete access-cost example; they are **not** used as training data or evaluation ground truth.

- **ShuttleAI / Shuttle Coach — Apple App Store.** Current listing describes practice-log AI feedback, video form analysis, rally tactics analysis and practice-menu suggestions.  
  https://apps.apple.com/sg/app/shuttleai-ai-badminton-coach/id6772496961
- **Badmi — AI analysis for badminton.** Current product page describes video analysis of footwork, swing and contact.  
  https://badmi.co/
- **Shuttle Ace Badminton Academy — lesson fees.** Current page lists one-to-one coaching at SGD 70/hour (coach), SGD 80/hour (senior coach) and SGD 100/hour (head coach).  
  https://www.shuttleacebadmintonacademy.com/classes-rates

## How this affects the project claim
The project does **not** claim to be the first AI badminton coach. Its narrower contribution is an auditable text-first prototype that explicitly routes a problem, retrieves from a small self-authored knowledge base, and requires generated advice to be traceable to retrieved notes.
